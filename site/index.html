How Distill works

Any single expert has blind spots. A rule that independent experts reach again and again is far more likely to hold up. Distill finds those rules, then digs out the principles underneath so you can adapt them instead of memorizing them.

  1. Frame the problem

    Restate it in one precise sentence and name the fields that study it.

  2. Map the canon

    Pick the 10 most authoritative sources, deliberately from different schools of thought.

  3. Extract the rules

    Pull about 10 actionable rules from each source, around 100 in total.

  4. Score the consensus

    Merge duplicates, count how many sources back each rule, and rank them. Disagreements are listed, not hidden.

  5. Find the roots

    Turn the top rules into steps for your situation, extract the 3 to 7 principles behind them, and build a technique of your own.

You finish with three rules to start this week and a check-in after 14 days. In Claude Code and Cowork, each source gets its own subagent, so the rules are extracted in one round.

Then ask in plain words: “Distill procrastination. I work full-time and only have evenings.”

Where Distill comes from

Distill grows out of an idea from one of Abdelkarim Ben Mohamadi's videos: to beat procrastination, read the 10 strongest books on it, extract their rules, keep the ones they share, and go back to the principles they grow from.

Distill applies the same five steps to any problem or skill, and adds safeguards so your AI doesn't invent sources along the way.
